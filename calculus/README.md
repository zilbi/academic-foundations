# Calculus

This section presents the main topics I studied in the university course **Calculus / Mathematical Analysis**, along with selected problems.

## Course Topics

### Real Numbers, Sets, and Sequences

* Sets, relations, and basic axioms of real numbers
* Mathematical induction
* Supremum and infimum
* Numerical sequences and their limits
* Comparison of growth rates of sequences
* Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem
* Cauchy sequences and the Cauchy convergence criterion
* Upper and lower limits of sequences
* Convergence and divergence of numerical sequences

### Limits and Continuity of Functions

* Equivalent definitions of the limit of a function
* One-sided limits
* Continuous functions and equivalent definitions of continuity
* Arithmetic operations with continuous functions
* Continuity of function composition
* Sign stabilization theorem
* Limit of sin(x) / x
* Power functions and their properties
* Uniform continuity

### Differential Calculus

* Derivative and differentiability at a point
* Geometric and physical meaning of the derivative
* Arithmetic operations with differentiable functions
* Derivative of a composition
* Derivative of an inverse function
* Derivatives of elementary functions
* Fermat's theorem
* Rolle's theorem
* Lagrange mean value theorem
* Cauchy mean value theorem
* Necessary and sufficient conditions for extrema
* Convex functions

### Taylor Formula and Expansions

* Taylor formula for polynomials and elementary functions
* Taylor expansions of sin(x), cos(x), and exp(x)
* Applications of Taylor expansions to limits and asymptotic estimates
* Irrationality of e
* Irrationality of pi

### Integral Calculus

* Antiderivative and indefinite integral
* Examples of functions without antiderivatives
* Substitution in indefinite integrals
* Integration by parts
* Definite integral
* Substitution in definite integrals
* Integral sums and estimates of approximation error
* Riemann integrability
* Trapezoidal rule
* Euler-Maclaurin formula for the second derivative

### Improper Integrals

* Improper integrals and their properties
* Absolute convergence
* Abel's test
* Dirichlet's test
* Integrals involving products of monotone and periodic functions

### Metric and Normed Spaces

* Metric spaces
* Induced metrics
* Scalar product and norm
* Limits in metric spaces: Cauchy and Heine definitions
* Cauchy criterion
* Continuous mappings between metric spaces
* Cantor theorem for mappings of metric spaces

### Curves and Arc Length

* Paths and their images
* Simple paths
* Smooth paths
* Length of a parametrically defined curve
* Length of the graph of a function
* Length of a curve in polar coordinates

### Numerical Series

* Series with nonnegative terms
* Comparison test
* Cauchy root test
* D'Alembert ratio test
* Absolute and conditional convergence
* Alternating series estimates

### Functional Sequences and Series

* Pointwise convergence of sequences of functions
* Uniform convergence of sequences of functions
* Criteria for uniform convergence
* Pointwise and uniform convergence of series of functions
* Necessary condition for uniform convergence of a series

### Power Series

* Power series
* Radius and interval of convergence
* Operations with power series
* Applications of power series to elementary functions

### Functions of Several Variables

* Partial derivatives
* Jacobian matrix
* Gradient
* Directional derivative
* Stationary points
* Local extrema
* Sufficient conditions for extrema
* Lagrange multipliers
* Distance from a point to a hyperplane
