# Discrete Mathematics

This section presents the main topics I studied in the university course **Discrete Mathematics**, along with selected problems.

## Course Topics

### Elementary Combinatorics

* Basic counting principles
* Sum and product rules
* Permutations and combinations
* Binomial coefficients
* Combinatorial identities
* Double counting
* Inclusion-exclusion principle
* Recurrence relations
* Classical counting problems
* Discrete probability and configuration counting

### Set Theory and Mathematical Language

* Sets and operations on sets
* Relations and functions
* Cartesian products
* Equivalence relations
* Partial orders
* Cardinality of finite and infinite sets
* Formal mathematical statements
* Proof techniques for discrete structures

### Mathematical Logic

* Propositional logic
* Boolean formulas
* Logical connectives
* Truth tables
* Tautologies and contradictions
* Logical equivalence
* Implication and proof structure
* Formalization of mathematical statements

### Elementary Graph Theory

* Graphs, vertices, and edges
* Directed and undirected graphs
* Paths, walks, trails, and cycles
* Connected components
* Trees and forests
* Degree of a vertex
* Handshaking lemma
* Bipartite graphs
* Shortest paths and minimal paths
* Centers and medians of graphs

### Connectivity, Trees, Cycles, and Cuts

* Spanning trees
* Minimum spanning trees
* Cut vertices and bridges
* Cuts and edge cuts
* Graph connectivity
* Edge connectivity
* Eulerian paths and cycles
* Hamiltonian paths and cycles

### Matchings, Colorings, and Planar Graphs

* Matchings in graphs
* Maximum matchings
* Perfect matchings
* Bipartite matchings
* Hall's marriage theorem
* Symmetric difference of matchings
* Graph coloring
* Chromatic number
* Planar graphs
* Euler formula for planar graphs
* Basic properties of planar embeddings

### Generating Functions and Recurrence Relations

* Ordinary generating functions
* Combinatorial interpretation of generating functions
* Operations on generating functions
* Linear recurrence relations
* Solving recurrence relations with generating functions
* Applications of generating functions to counting problems

### Finite Fields and Coding Theory

* Modular arithmetic
* Finite fields
* Polynomial arithmetic over finite fields
* Error-detecting and error-correcting codes
* Basic coding theory bounds
* Reed-Solomon codes
* BCH codes
* Basic encoding and decoding ideas
